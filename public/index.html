About

Planning made calm, clear and personal

Organising a celebration means juggling money, dates, vendors and a hundred small decisions. Celebration Planner turns what you know about your event into a plan you can follow, so nothing important is left until the last minute.

It covers weddings, birthdays and community events, and adapts to your guest count, budget and the time you have left. An optional Gemini AI assistant adds fresh ideas and answers questions, and a built-in planner keeps everything working without it.

Features

Everything you need to host with confidence

  • Smart budget

    Amounts always add up to your total. Pick up to three priorities and keep a protected reserve.

  • Dated timeline

    Tasks are counted back from your event date and compress automatically when time is short.

  • Vendor checklist

    Booking deadlines in order of lead time, with the questions to ask each vendor.

  • Personal ideas

    Ideas ranked by your theme and interests, and filtered by guest count and budget.

  • Ask the planner

    Get quick answers about your plan, from the AI assistant or the built-in planner.

  • Private and accessible

    Plans stay in your browser. The design supports keyboard use, screen readers and reduced motion.

  • Spending tracker

    Record what you spend in each category and see what is left, with a clear warning in words when a category goes over budget.

  • Print and download

    Download your plan as a text file or print every section at once, ready to share with family, vendors or your team.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is my plan stored, and who can see it?

Your plan is stored only in your own browser, using the local storage feature that every modern browser includes. When you choose Create my plan, your event details are sent to the planner's server so that it can build the budget, timeline and vendor checklist, but the server does not save them or write them to its logs. Nobody else can open your plan, and there is no account to create. Clearing your browser data, or choosing Clear saved plan on the Plan an event page, removes it completely. The trade-off is that a plan lives in one browser, so it will not appear on another device unless you download it as a text file and keep that copy.

How is my budget divided between categories?

Each event type starts from a sensible split across categories such as venue, food and drink, decor, music, photography and invitations. A contingency reserve is taken off the top first, because prices and plans nearly always change, and the rest is shared out in proportion to those defaults. If some areas matter most to you, choose up to three budget priorities and they receive a larger share while the reserve stays the same. The amounts are always whole numbers that add up exactly to the total you entered, and you can enter what you actually spend in each category to see how much is left and when a category has gone over.

What happens if my event is only a few weeks away?

The planner works out how many days you have between today and your event date. If that is shorter than the lead time normally recommended for your kind of event, it compresses the timeline so that every task still falls between today and the event while keeping the tasks in the right order, and it tells you plainly that it has done so. Tasks in the final week and after the event keep their normal timing where possible. When time is short, start with the vendors that have the longest lead times, such as the venue, the caterer and the photographer, because the best options are often booked first.

Do I need the AI assistant to use the planner?

No. The built-in planner creates your budget, timeline, vendor checklist and ideas, and it answers common questions about your plan on its own, so everything works without any AI service. When the AI assistant is switched on for the site, you also get a button to generate fresh ideas and richer answers to your questions, written from a short description of your event. In that case the description and your question are sent to the AI provider, and if the assistant is unavailable or answers badly, the built-in planner steps in. Every idea and answer is labelled, so you always know which one produced it.
